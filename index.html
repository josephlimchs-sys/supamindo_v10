Community Story

Life at Block 302

Block 302 is home to many families from different backgrounds, cultures and professions. Like many HDB neighbourhoods in Singapore, it is a place where neighbours greet one another, children grow up together and everyday conversations happen in lifts, void decks and coffee shops.

One of the residents is Roy, an ICT Manager who works in a primary school. He is passionate about helping children understand AI literacy and the importance of balancing screen time with real-life experiences.

Bao Jin Tian is a dedicated tuition teacher who enjoys helping students improve their studies. Christine is a successful career professional who values simple pleasures, while Muthu enjoys a slower pace of life and friendly conversations at the neighbourhood coffee shop.

Nurani is passionate about protecting the environment and helping her family develop healthy habits. Through everyday conversations and small acts of care, the residents of Block 302 create a community where everyone looks out for one another.

Life at Block 302 is not just about living under the same roof. It is about building friendships, sharing experiences and learning from one another.
